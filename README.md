### Featured Project — Final Year Design Project

**Impact of Land Use/Land Cover Change on Flash Floods: A Hydrological Modeling Approach in the Soan River Watershed, Rawalpindi-Islamabad**
*Advisor: Lt Colonel Muhammad Ali Khan · 2026 – Present*

- Classified Landsat imagery into LULC maps (Waterbody, Built-up, Vegetation, Barren land) for 2000–2024 at 4-year intervals using Random Forest in Google Earth Engine.
- Built a Random Forest–Cellular Automata (RF-CA) model in Python to predict LULC for 2032, 2040, 2048, and 2056, validated against the classified 2020 and 2024 maps.
- Now comparing present-day and predicted runoff through hydrological modeling to see how peak flow and surface runoff are expected to change.
